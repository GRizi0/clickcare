Part B: Compare Before You Buy

The listed price of a product is not always the final cost. Before choosing a product, compare the base price, shipping fee, total cost, and return policy. The example below uses the fictional products from Part A.

Product Listed Price Shipping Fee Total Cost Return Policy
ClearSound Headphones ₱1,299.00 ₱80.00 ₱1,379.00 7 days (defective only)
TrailReady Backpack ₱850.00 ₱100.00 ₱950.00 Damaged items only, 48 hrs
HydroKeep Bottle ✔ ₱420.00 ₱60.00 ₱480.00 15 days, free return shipping

💡 What This Example Shows

The HydroKeep Bottle has the lowest total cost and the most consumer-friendly return policy — free returns within 15 days for any reason. Even if another product had a slightly lower listed price, a high shipping fee or a restrictive return policy could make it a worse deal overall.

Always calculate the total cost and read the return policy before confirming your purchase.
