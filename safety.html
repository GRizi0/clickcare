🛡️ Shopping Safety

Practical tips to help you shop online with confidence and avoid scams.

Online Shopping Safety Tips

Following these basic practices can significantly reduce your risk when shopping online.

✔ Check Seller Ratings and Customer Feedback

Before buying, read recent reviews from verified buyers. Look for consistent feedback about product quality, delivery time, and seller communication. Be cautious of sellers with very few reviews or only five-star ratings with no written comments.

✔ Review Product Descriptions and Photos Carefully

Read the full product description, including size, material, and specifications. Check if the photos are clear and realistic. Vague descriptions or stock photos that do not match the product are warning signs.

✔ Use Secure and Trusted Payment Methods

Prefer payment methods that offer buyer protection, such as credit cards, debit cards, or platform-based payment systems (e.g., GCash, PayMaya, or platform wallets). Avoid direct bank transfers to unknown sellers whenever possible.

✔ Avoid Suspicious Links and Unexpected Messages

Do not click links in unsolicited text messages or emails claiming you won a prize or that your account needs verification. Always go directly to the official website or app instead of following links from unknown sources.

✔ Check Shipping Fees and Return Policies Before Paying

Always review the total cost, including shipping fees, before confirming your order. Understand the return and refund policy in case the item arrives damaged or is not as described.

✔ Keep Personal and Financial Information Private

Never share your passwords, PINs, OTPs, or full card details with sellers or through chat messages. Legitimate platforms will never ask for this information outside of their secure checkout process.

How to Identify Fraudulent Online Stores

Scammers often create fake stores or listings that look convincing at first glance. Watch out for these common warning signs:

⚠ Prices That Seem Unrealistically Low

If a product is priced far below its typical market value — for example, a branded smartphone for a few hundred pesos — it is almost certainly a scam or a counterfeit item.

⚠ Missing Contact Details or Business Information

Legitimate sellers provide clear contact information, a business name, and a way to reach them if there is a problem. A store with no address, no contact number, and no verifiable identity is a red flag.

⚠ Fake or Repetitive Customer Reviews

Watch for reviews that all sound the same, use very similar language, or were all posted within a short period. These may be purchased or fabricated reviews designed to mislead buyers.

⚠ Pressure to Pay Immediately

Scammers often create a false sense of urgency — "Only 1 left!", "Offer expires in 10 minutes!" — to pressure you into paying before you have time to think or verify the seller.

⚠ Requests for Passwords, PINs, or OTPs

No legitimate seller, bank, or payment platform will ever ask you to share your OTP, PIN, or password through chat, text, or email. If someone asks for these, stop the transaction immediately.

⚠ Unusual Payment Requests or Suspicious Website Addresses

Be cautious if a seller insists on payment through an unusual method (e.g., gift cards, cryptocurrency, or direct remittance). Also check the website URL — scam sites often use addresses that closely mimic legitimate ones with small spelling differences.

What to Do if You Suspect a Scam

If something feels wrong during an online transaction, act quickly and carefully.

  1. Stop the transaction. Do not proceed with payment if you have doubts about the seller or the listing.
  2. Do not share additional personal information. Providing more details will only increase your risk.
  3. Save relevant receipts and messages. Take screenshots of the listing, your conversation with the seller, and any payment confirmations as evidence.
  4. Report the seller through the shopping platform. Most platforms (Shopee, Lazada, Facebook Marketplace, etc.) have a reporting feature for fraudulent sellers.
  5. Contact your payment provider if money was sent. If you already paid, contact your bank, e-wallet provider, or payment platform immediately to report the transaction and ask about dispute options.

📌 Remember

Reporting scams helps protect other consumers. Even if you cannot recover your money, your report may prevent others from being victimized by the same seller.
